# Warriner et al. (2013) affective norms

`Ratings_Warriner_et_al.csv` is distributed here unmodified, as obtained from the
secondary distribution at https://github.com/JULIELab/XANEW.

Source: Warriner, A. B., Kuperman, V., & Brysbaert, M. (2013). Norms of valence,
arousal, and dominance for 13,915 English lemmas. *Behavior Research Methods*, 45,
1191–1207.
